# Refund Policy

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refund requests are submitted through the billing portal under Settings > Billing > Request refund.

Annual plans that are cancelled after 30 days are refunded on a prorated basis for the unused months. Monthly plans are not refundable after the billing period starts.

Refunds are processed within 5 business days and return to the original payment method.
